Coming soon to Google Play

Black Splatter!

A dark action platformer for Android from All Ties Severed Productions, LLC.

You are Splatter, an ethereal being who fights demons and aliens across hostile, biomechanical worlds. The goo that drips from you is more than a trail — it is how you slip between dimensional planes.

Jump the pits. Arm up. Face bosses that do not play fair. Three surreal stages and an original ambient soundtrack, composed by Jon Castaneda of All Ties Severed, carry you from living machinery to bone-strewn works and streets of ash.

Free to play, with an optional upgrade

Black Splatter! will be free on Google Play, supported by ads. An optional in-app purchase, the Reinforced Suit (Premium), removes ads, adds protection on later stages, and grants an extra life on each stage attempt.
