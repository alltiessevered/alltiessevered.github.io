The band

All Ties Severed

Psychedelic, electronic, punk, industrial, and folk — written and performed by Jon Castaneda, Bri Rodriquez, and Adan Baca in El Paso, Texas.

Formed around 2000, All Ties Severed builds soundscapes that move from the personal to the hypnotic. Live shows are meant to be immersive: surreal projections, creative props, and visuals folded into the set so the music has somewhere to go besides the speakers.

Jon also composed the original ambient soundtrack for Black Splatter!, the studio’s upcoming Android game.
